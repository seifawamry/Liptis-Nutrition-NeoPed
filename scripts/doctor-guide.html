Standard Preparation & Reconstitution Protocol

1. Standard Dilution

15.0% w/v concentration

15.0g powder in 90 mL sterile water yields 100 mL prepared feed (caloric density 79.7 kcal/100 mL).

2. Scoop Calibration

5.0 grams per level scoop

Use only the enclosed manufacturer scoop. Level with a sterile spatula without compressing or packing powder.

3. Safe Reconstitution

> 70°C sterile boiled water

Dissolve powder in sterile water >70°C per WHO/FAO PIF guidelines. Cool to feeding temperature before administration.
